Tech skills for everyone.
Mentors who show the way.

Tech for Women is a nonprofit bringing tech education to underserved students across the Valley. We reach communities that don't have the opportunity yet, through free workshops and classes that anyone can join.

This site makes connecting easier. Mentors and mentees can meet online, talk by call, or get together in person, and share posts and questions with the community.

  • 💬Connect your way: online, by call, or in person

  • 🌵Built for our community in the Valley
